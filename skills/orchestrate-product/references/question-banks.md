# Question banks (ask in Russian, record answers in English docs)

Ask in batches of 5–8. Skip what the user already answered. Dig deeper on vague answers.

## Batch A — Problem & audience

1. What are we building in one sentence?
2. Who is the primary user? (role, context)
3. What painful job does this solve today?
4. How do they solve it now? Why is that bad?
5. What does success look like in 30 days after launch?
6. Competitors / references they like (links)?
7. References they dislike (and why)?

## Batch B — Scope

1. Must-have for v1 (list)?
2. Explicit non-goals for v1?
3. Nice-to-have later?
4. Multi-page site vs single landing vs app with accounts?
5. User-generated content? Admin panel?
6. i18n? Primary language?
7. Mobile: responsive only or native later?

## Batch C — Constraints

1. Preferred stack or "you choose best practice"?
2. Hosting preference (Vercel, own VPS, static, unknown)?
3. Auth required? Providers?
4. Payments / subscriptions?
5. CMS / blog?
6. Analytics?
7. Deadline and rough complexity budget (MVP vs polished)?
8. Accessibility level (baseline WCAG 2.2 AA vs best-effort)?
9. Brand: colors, fonts, logo, tone — or invent?

## Batch D — Content & trust

1. Real copy available or draft OK?
2. Real images / stock / generate?
3. Legal pages needed (privacy, terms)?
4. Contact / lead capture destination (email, CRM, form backend)?
5. SEO priorities (keywords, locales)?

## Batch E — Quality & delivery

1. Tests: unit / e2e required?
2. CI expected?
3. Deploy as part of this run?
4. Who maintains after handoff?
5. Acceptance checklist in their words?

## Soft probes (use when answers are thin)

- "If we cut half the scope, what stays?"
- "What would make you reject the result?"
- "Show me three sites whose *feel* you want (not features)."
- "What must never break?"

## Question format (grill-me)

For each item in a batch, prefer:

```text
N. <вопрос>
   Рекомендация: <ваш лучший ответ / вариант A>
   Альтернативы: B … / C …
```

User can reply «ок» / «B» / short correction. Faster than blank essays.
